The Challenge

The "Black Box" Enigma

An anonymous legacy software firm has challenged the community to breach their custom "unbreakable" file encryption tool. The source code was lost years ago, leaving only the compiled executable.

Your mission is to reverse engineer this binary. You must map its execution flow, identify the hardcoded cryptographic keys, write a patch to bypass the license check, and ultimately build a custom decryptor to extract the hidden payload inside.

Rules of Engagement

Strict guidelines to ensure a fair and secure competition.

1

Team Structure

Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members. Cross-college teams are permitted provided at least one member is from MPSTME.

2

No Network Attacks

This is a local reverse engineering challenge. Any DDoS attacks, scanning, or attempts to breach ACM infrastructure will result in immediate disqualification.

3

Original Documentation

Plagiarism of write-ups is strictly prohibited. Teams must submit their own unique documentation detailing their methodology and step-by-step logic.

Frequently Asked Questions

I have no prior experience in reverse engineering, can I still join?

Yes! While it is a challenging domain, we will provide basic workshops and resources before the hackathon begins to get you up to speed.

Will food and accommodation be provided?

Absolutely. All participants will be provided with meals, snacks, and resting areas for the entire 24-hour duration.

What tools are we allowed to use?

You may use any standard RE tools including IDA Pro (Free/Edu), Ghidra, x64dbg, Wireshark, and custom Python scripts.

Is there a registration fee?

Registration is completely free for MPSTME students. External participants have a nominal platform fee of ₹200 per team.
